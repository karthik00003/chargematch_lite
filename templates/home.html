Our Services

Every rule below is enforced inside the database by triggers, procedures and constraints.

ChargeMatch connects EV drivers with people who have a charger at home. Hosts list their charger with a price and opening hours; an admin approves each one. Drivers find a charger that fits their vehicle, send a booking request, and pay the host for the energy actually used.

Find Chargers

Search approved home chargers near you on the map, with opening hours and amenities.

Smart Compatibility Check

Only chargers whose plug fits your vehicle and whose parking bay fits its size are shown.

Fair Billing

The host records the battery % at start and end; the database calculates kWh × price and the bill can't be changed.
